Tamanna Hai Ab Jaa Ke Jismann Milu'n
Magar Dil Toh Pehle Fidaa Hogaya
Bazaahir Yahaa'n Jism Toh Reh Gaya
Haqeeqat Mein Sab Kuch Juda Hogaya


Koi Lamha Aisa Guzar Ta Nahi'n
Mai'n Jis Mein Unhe Yaad Karta Nahi'n
Mujhe Unki Baaton Ne Kya Kardiya 
Kahun Kya Ke Mai'n Kya Se Kya Hogaya


Wahi Ilm Mein Hai Wahi Fehm Mein
Wahi Zikr Mein Hai Wahi Fikr Mein 
Mere Nooria Tujh Se Mai'n Kya Huwa
Khudaa Ki Qasam Ba Khuda Hogaya


Mere Dil Ki Duniya Badalti Gayi
Jo Thi Baat Bigdi Voh Banti Gayi
Meri Zindagaani Sawarti Gayi 
Tu Harr Dam Mera Rehnuma Hogaya 


Tere Faiz Se Mujhko Sab Kuch Mila
Ye Duniya Mili Dou Jahaa'n Mil Gaya
Mile Kyun Na Dono Jahaa'n 
Mere Jab Nabi Hogaye Aur Khudaa Hogaya


Kisi Ko Junoo'n Mera Aata Nazar 
Kisi Ko Sukoo'n Mera Aata Nazar
Khudaa Ko Daru'n Mera Aata Nazar
Ab Anta Ana Khud Huwa Hogaya 


Mujhe Zindagi Ka Pata Mil Gaya 
Mujhe Bandagi Ka Mazaa Mil Gaya
Ke Jis Aan Shaikh-E-Safaa Mil Gaya
Mai'n Aaiena-E-Aaenama Hogaya
