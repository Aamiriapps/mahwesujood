Tu Shamaa Hai Mehfil Noorani
Hum Shamaa Ke Parwaane Nikle

Ek Noor Barasta Hai Tujh Se
Noori Tere Dewaane Nikle

Haqq Noor Hai Aur Tu Noori Hai
Zulmath Ko Tujhi Se Doori Hai

Anwaar Ke Sa-ghar Pee Pee Kar
Saaqi Tere Mastaane Nikle 

Barsoon Mein Jo Ban-na Mushkil Hai
Vo Kaam Banna Tujh Se Aise

Chand Lamhoon Ki Baarish Tujh Se
Jo Lee A'alam Pe Vo Barrsaane Nikle

Jis Jaa Bhi Gaye Hai'n Tere Qadam
Anwaar Ki Baarish Hai Pae-Ham

Zulmath Gaye Noor Aisa Chaaya 
Har Simth Se Parwane Nikle

Alfaaz Hai'n Tere Rehmaan 
Afa'al Hai'n Tere Wijdaani

Har Lafz Ke Hai'n Sou Sou Maani
Har Fehm Ke Pae-Maane Nikle

Kya Nazr Karu'n Aaqa Teri
Jaa'n Bhi Hai Nahi Baaqi Meri

Mai Tera Hu Jitne Mere Hai
Shahaa Tere Nazrane Nikle

Har Wasf Tera Noorani Hai
Aur Zaat Teri Laasaani Hai

Ab Dil Mein Ye Hum Ne Thaani
Hai A'alam Kahe Deewane Nikle

Jab Shamae Tariqath Bujh Ne Lage
Har Simth Mein Zulmath Chaane Lage

Jal Uthe Wahi'n Shamae Noori
Har Qalb Ko Garmaane Nikle

Sab Kuch Mile Aamir Noori Se
Allah Mile Toh Kya Na Mile

Bas Uski Razaa Ki Hajat Hai
Le Kar Yahi Deewane Nikle
