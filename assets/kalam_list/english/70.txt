
Pareshaa'n Hoo Na Bande 
Harr Tarah Allah Kafi Hai 
Sabab Faani Musabbib Harr 
Dam-O-Harr Aan Baqi Hai 

Teri Kotahiyoo'n Ki Thi Saza 
Jo Darr Ba Darr Bhatka 
Arey Nadaa'n Voh Aqrab 
Az Rag-E-Jaa'n Tera Saathi Hai

Usi Ka Saz-o-Samaa'n Hai 
Usi Ka Sab Yeh Faizaa'n Hai 
Jo Uske Ghair Ka Ya Apna 
Samjhe Ga Voh Baaghi Hai 

Hata A'alam Ko Nazroon Se 
Mita Sab Zehn Ke Naqshe 
Wahi Toh Qalb-E-Momin Ke 
Liye Bas Ek Haadi Hai

Samandar Mein Nahi'n Tangi 
Magar Hai Zarf Mein Tangi 
Musabbib Sab Mein Aali Hai 
Sabab Kam Zarf Paapi Hai 

Kuchal De Zarf Ko Apne 
Masal De Qalb Ko Apne 
Utar Ja Behr Mein Uske 
Tu Phir Paaki He Paaki Hai

Voh Haadi Qalb-E-Momin Ka 
Muzill Hai Qalb-E-Faasiq Ka
Jisay Taqwa Hai Vo Tairea 
Voh Doobe Ga Jo Taaghi Hai

Ghani Hai Toh Khudaa He Hai
Dhani Hai Toh Khudaa Hi Hai
Khudaa Se Le Ke Inn Ko De 
Miyaa'n Yeh Kaam Aali Hai

Usse Ro Ro Ke Main Ne Jab 
Pukara Toh Kaha Aamir
Mera Hai Woh Jo Noori 
Hai Nahin Uska Jo Naari Hai
