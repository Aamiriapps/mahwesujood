 
Mujh Jaise Burae Par Baabae Karam
Ab Tak Jo Khula Hai Kya Bolu'n
Karta Hu'n Bura Aur Hu'n Bhi Bura
Phir Fazl-E-Khudaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Hota Nahi'n Mujhse Khair Koi
Aur Sharr Ki Toh Koi Hadd Na Rahi
Phir Rehmo Karam Unka Mujh Par
Auroo'n Se Siva Hai Kya Bolu'n

Shunwa Hai Wahi Sunta Hai
Harr Ek Ki Dua Ko Sham-O-Seher
Uske Siva Uske Bandoo'n Se
Kehna He Khataa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Aa Tujhko Bataoo'n Raaz-E-Nihaa'n
Jaata Hai Kahan Aa Sab Hai Yahaa'n
Allah Jahaa'n Hai Sab Hai Wahaa'n
Kya Cheez Judaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Baat Itni Hai Isko Paana Hai
Phir Isme Sabhi Kuch Paana Hai
Aafaaq Mein Vo Hai Bann Ke Huwa
Anfus Mein Ana Hai Kya Bolun

Samaan Nahi Rakte Hai Magar
Hum Khaaliq-E-Samaa'n Rakte Hai
Ab Humse Bhala Kya Hona Sakae
Aur Kya Na Huwa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Ye Voh Hai Faqeeri Sun Le Miyaa
Shahoo'n Se Bhi Unche Hum Hai Yahaa'n
Iss Masnad-E-Fakhre Aali Ka 
Sultan Gadaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Apnoo'n Ko Bhi Tu He Deta Hai
Ghairoo'n Ka Bhi Tu He Daata Hai
Bas Tere Karam Par Harr Aa'n
Ab Dil Mera Fidaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Tu Hamd Ke Layaq Hai Baeshaq
Alfaaz Nahi Paas Mere
Khaasir Hai Zabaa'n Aur Khama Mera
Kya Hamd-O-Sana Hai Kya Bolu'n

Tu Khaaliq-E-Jism-O-Jaan-O-Dil
Tu Naafiq-E-Jaa'n Darr Aabo Gil
Kehna Toh Ye Aata Hai Mujhko
Kis Tarah Kya Hai Kya Bolu'n

Bae Paani Ke Maahi Je Na Sakae
Bae Saans Ke Baadi Rehna Sakae
Pathar Mein Jilaya Keede Ko
Kis Tarah Jiya Hai Kya Bolu'n

Har Cheez Hai Aslan Mitti Ki
Mitti Mein Mazaa Bu Rang Kahan
Harr Phal Ka Mazaa Harr Gul Ki Mehek
Harr Shakl Judaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Ab Tak Jo Huwa Hai Fazl-E-Khudaa
Bas Itna Bataa Kya Shukr Kiya
Iss Par Bhi Voh Teri Sunta Hai
Kya Mera Khudaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Ab Usko Suna Jo Shunwa Hai
Aur Maang Wahi Ek Daata Hai
Kya Tujhko Na Dega Aye Bande
Jo Tera Khudaa Hai Kya Bolu'n

Voh Kaam Jo Ain-E-Mansha Hai
Inka Hai Miyaa'n Tu Jinka Hai
Aamir Tera Sab Kuch Samaa'n Ab
Noori Ki Dua Hai Kya Bolu'n
