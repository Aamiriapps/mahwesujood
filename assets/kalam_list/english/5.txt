Tumhi Par Raat Din Shayda 
Mere Aaqa Mera Dil Hai
Bahut Baetaab Hun Dard-E
-Jigar Se Haal-E-Bismil Hai

Koi Hoo Ya Na Hoo Tum Hoo
Tumhari He Mujhe Dhun Hai
Tumhare Ghair Ka Ab Tazkara
Bhi Samme Qaatil Hai

Jahaa'n Se Aur Jahaa'n Waloon
Se Mujh Ko Vaasta Kya Hai
Kise Ko Fikr-E-Rozi Hai
Koi Mehw-E-Masha Ghal Hai

Kisi Ko Kya Sunaoon Aur 
Koi Kya Sunn Sake Meri
Tumhare Maasiwa  Mein Kaun 
Kuch Sunne Ke Khaabil Hai

Jo Behr-E-Maasiwa Hai 
Isme Ab Harr Dam Talaatum Hai
Sukoon-O-Amn Ka Mere
Liye Tera He Saahil Hai

Koi Taalib Hai Duniya Ka
Koi Taalib Hai Ukhba Ka
Mai Kiski Hun Talab Mein
Dekh Loo Haazir Mera Dil Hai

Agr Doo Ya Na Doo Marzi 
Tumhari Tum Toh Maalik Hoo
Magar Iss Aastaane Se 
Na Jaaye Ga Vo Saahil Hai

Koi Ab Qaabiliyat  Mujh Mein
Baaqi He Nahi'n Aaqa
Tawasutt Ke Liye Noori Piya
Bas Ek Qaabil Hai

Ussi Ke Vaste Se Kuch 
Meri Ab Sunn Bhi Le Maula
Toh Bole Aamira Ye Baat
Bas Sune Ke Khaabil Hai
