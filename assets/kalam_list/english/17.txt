
Sab Se Ab Choot Ke Hum 
Iss Se Lage Baithe Hain 
Kya Khabar Kis Ko Ke Hum 
Kis Se Mile Baithe Hain

Aaye Koi Ke Na Aaye 
Hume Parwa Kya Hai
Hum Do A'alam Se Nazar 
Bandh Ke Baithe Hain 

Allahu Allahu Se Lutf 
Ajab Aane Lagaa
Zikr Ke Kaif Ki Lazzath 
Liye Baithe Hain 

Laakh Jalwath Hoo Fidaa 
Khalwath-E-Janaa Pe Yaqeen
Mai Wahdath Ka Liye 
Jaam Piye Baithe Hain

Haech Aafath Na Rasad 
Ghoushaye Tanhayee Raa
Khaal Tha Saahib-E-Ehwaal 
Banne Baithe Hain

Dene Waale Tere Dene 
Ke Mai Sadqe Jaon
Jaan-O-Dil Jism Diye 
Jin Se Jiye Baithe Hain

Yaad Ki Baat Hoo 
Ya Yaft Ki Halat Koi
Lab Kushayi Hai Kahaa'n 
Hont Siye Baithe Hain

Aafatie'n Aati Hain Par 
Halki Se Halki Hokar
Sarr Se Tal Jaati Hain Par
Hum Toh Jamei'n Baithe Hain

Jab Tawakkul Hai Toh 
Ghairoo'n Ko Sunana Kya Hai
Rabbuna Allah Toh Kehna 
Tha Kahe Baithe Hain

Hukm Aane Se Bhi Pehle 
Se Hum Aamil Nikle
Faiz-E-Noori Ko Liye 
Aur Jiye Baithe Hain

Ab Bhala Kis Ko Koi 
Chahega Aamir Kehna
Ba Khudaa Jab Ke Khudaa 
He Se Mile Baithe Hain
