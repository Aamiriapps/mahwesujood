Mujhe Unki Yaad Hai Raat Din
Mujhe Ab Unhi Ka Khayal Rahe
Na Jahaa'n Ki Dil Mein Hai Aaruzu
Na Kise Se Mera Sawaal Hai

Koi Khud Parasti Ka Mann Chala
Koi Budd Parasti Mein Mubtala
Mai'n Khudaa Ki Kha Ke Qasam Kahun
Na Ye Khurb Hai Na Visaal Hai

Koi Maal Ke Hai Khayal Mein
Koi Ishq-E-Husn-O-Jamaal Mein
Na Kise Ke Paas Yeh Reh Sake
Inn Hae Raat Din Ka Zawaal Hai

Kab Mujhse Puch Haqeeqatein 
Vo Ho'n Naar Ki Ke Nayeem Ki
Mere Yaar Ki Doo Tajaleyaa'n
Vo Jalaal Hai Ye Jamaal Hai

Tera Naam Leke Jo Nooria Mai'n 
Ne Maanga Uss Se Vo Dediya
Kahan Aamira Tu Na Ranj Kar
Humei'n Kab Na Tera Khayal Hai
