
Kisi Ka Aaine Haqq Hona
Haqeeqat Isko Kehte Hai
Jo Hai Usko He Hai Kehna 
Sadaqat Usko Kehte Hai

Koi Apna Samaj Ta Hai 
Koi Haqq Ka Samajta Hai
Qiyanath Isko Kehte Hai
Amanath Usko Kehte Hai

Mera Aur Tera Ahl-E-Haqq 
Bhi Kehte Hai Zaroorat Parr
Izafath Khalq Ki Haqq Ki
Asalath Isko Kehte Hai

Hamesha Haqq Ko Haazir
Aur Naazir Samajte Hai
Khudi Kho Karr Isse Dekhe
Shahadath Usko Kehte Hai

Nazar Mein Khalq Reh Jaaye
Ya Issmein Haqq Nazar Aaye
Basarat Isko Kehte Hai
Baseerat Isko Kehte Hai

Daraata Jaabiroo'n Ko Tu
Hasaata Jaabhuloo'n Ko Tu
Nazarath Isko Kehte Hai
Basharath Isko Kehte Hai

Koi Haqq Ki Talab Mein Hai
Kisi Mein Haqq Numai Hai
Iradath Isko Kehte Hai
Maasheqath Isko Kehte Hai

Mahaamid Hai Khudaa Ke Vaaste
Saare Maratib Mein
Sataish Khud Ke Jo Chahe 
Himakhat Isko Kehte Hai

Agar Banna Hai Kuch Tujh
Ko Khudi Ko Mitata Ja
Khudaa Waale Taraqi Ke
Aalamat Isko Kehte Hai

Tujhe Fitrat Ne Jo Kuch
De Diya Tujh Ko Mubarak Hoo
Baja Masruf Ho Garr Uska
Fazelat Usko Kehte Hai

Khudi Ko Dur Karne Ki
Hamesha Soch Mein Reh Ja
Mashaqat Usko Kehte Hai
Riyazat Usko Kehte Hai

Tujhe Aamir Jahaa'n Bhi
Jis Tarah Bhi Jo Bhi Milta Hai
Ye Faizaan-E-Noori Aur
Himayath Isko Kehte Hai
