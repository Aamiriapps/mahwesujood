 
Meri Aankhoon Mei'n Tu He Aagaya 
Mai Jidhar Gaya Udhar Aagaya
Mujhe Ab Na Koi Khabar Rahi
Mai Kidhar Gaya Kidhar Aagaya

Na He Manziloo'n Ki Khabar Mujhe
Na He Mehfiloo'n Pe Nazar Meri
Ke Jidhar Bhi Meri Nazar Uthi
Tu Nazar Mai Sar Basar Aagaya

Koi Puchta Hai Tera Pataa Koi 
Tujh Ko Kehta Hai Laa Pataa
Mera Haal-O-Khaal Yahi Kahe
Nazar Aagaya Nazar Aagaya

Kabhi Dekhta Hun Namaz Mei'n
Kabhi Parda Haae Majaz Mei'n
Ke Jidhar Bhi Maine Hai Rukh Kiya
Tera Rukh Mujhe Nazar Aagaya

Jahaan Khalq Saamne Aagayi
Ke Mai Kya Kahun Ke Kahaan Gayi
Tu Chupa Toh Mai Nazar Aagaya
Mai Chupa Toh Tu Nazar Aagaya

Kahi'n Ishq Mei'n Kahi'n Husn Mei'n
Kahi'n Sabr Mei'n Kahi'n Jabr Mei'n
Kahi'n Naaz Mei'n Ya Niyaaz Mei'n
Tu Jahaan Raha Nazar Aagaya

Mere Harr Maqam Mein Tu Rahaa
Meri Subha Vo Shaam Mein Tu Rahaa
Mere Raat Din Ke Qiyaam Mei'n
Mai Jidhar Gaya Udhar Aagaya

Meri Zindagi Ka Madar Tu 
Meri Zindagi Ka Vikhaar Tu 
Tu Agar Gaya Toh Mai Marr Gaya
Meri Zindagi Tu Girr Aagaya

Mai Adam Ka Aaina Bann Gaya
Tu Khidam Ka Jalwa Numa Banna
Meri Tu Nazar Mei'n Hai Raat Din
Tu Jahaan Rahaa Nazar Aagaya

Tere Rehne Ki Ye Jagah Hai Bas
Mai Hun Faani Teri Baqaa Hai Sab
Mere Dil He Ek Jagah Hai Tu
Jahaan Nooria Nazar Aagaya

Wahi Rehnuma Wahi Haqqnuma
Wahi Aaina Banna Aienama
Tera Aamir Wahi Aasra 
Tu Jidhar Rahaa Udhar Aagaya
