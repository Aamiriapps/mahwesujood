
Tumhari Yaad Ka Harr Dam
Mere Dil Mein Ujaala Hai
Issi Se Harr Jagah Harr 
Taur Mera Bol Baala Hai

Meri Aahoo'n Ko Kya Dekhe 
Koi Aur Dard Kya Samjhe
Wahi Jaane Jise Souz-E-Jigar
Hai Jalne Walaa Hai

Huwa Mujhse Nahi Haqq Teri
Ta-aath Ka Adaa Bilkul 
Kahan Mujh Jaise Naaqis Se
Bhala Kuch Hone Walaa Hai

Bhalai Sarr Basar Tujh Mein 
Burai Sarr Basar Mujh Mein
Tera Ehsaan Hai Aise
Burae Ko Jo Sambhala Hai

Koi Zarra Nahi'n A'alam Ka
Jis Se Tu Nahi Zaahir
Zamaane Mai Toh Hai Lekin
Zamaane Se Nirala Hai

Tu Voh Zaahir Hai Jis Parr
Harr Taraf Mazhar Ka Hai Parde
Hai Mazhar Aaina Jis Mein
Tu Zaahir Hone Waala Hai

Diya Vo Hai Ke Jo Khud 
Dusre Ko Bhi Diya Karde
Issi Noor-E-Basirat Ka 
Wilayat Mein Ujala Hai

Ussi Sham-E-Nabuwat Ko
Tu Apne Shaeq Se Lele
Hataa De Zulmatei'n Kasrat 
Tu Wehdath Ka Ujala Hai

Koi Kya Jaane Iske Raaz
Kya Hai Aur Vo Kya Hai
Ke Jo Adrak-E-Bashari Se
Hazaroo'n Taur Baala Hai

Kahaan Dhondega Isko Inn
Zamaanoo'n Mein Makaanoo'n Mein
Kahan Tak Tujh Ko Fursat Hai
Kahan Tak Jeene Walaa Hai

Makaa'n Zaahir Aur Baatin Mein
Zamaa'n Awwal-O-Aakhir Mein
Huwe Gum Toh Bataa Iss Ke 
Siva Kya Rehne Waala Hai

Hazaroon Dukh Uthaye Aur
Hazaroon Zakhm Bhi Khaye
Magar Uss Ki Mohabbat Ko 
Jigar Ke Khoon Se Paala Hai
