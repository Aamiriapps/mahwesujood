
Mere Amal Ka Na Maloom Kya Sila Hoga
Jahannami Bhi Koi Mujh Se Kya Bura Hoga

Amal Pe Kuch Nahi Daar-O-Madaar Rehmat Ka
Tumhare Fazl-O-Karam Se Mera Bhala Hoga

Mila Jo Kuch Hai Voh Tere Karam Ka Sadqa Hai 
Wagarna Hum Se Faqeeron Ka Haal Kya Hoga

Madaar-E-Zindagi Zaahir Mein Bas Hawa Par Hai
Ye Ruk Gayi Toh Koi Lamha Bhar Jiya Hoga

Agar Na Shams-O-Qamar Hon Agar Na Hon Taare
Jale Ga Kya Voh Jo Makhlooq Ka Diya Hoga

Hazaar Baar Burai Ki Maine Koshish Ki
Tera Karam Jo Bachaye Toh Kab Bura Hoga

Jahaa'n Ne Iska Harr Ek Taur Se Buraa Chaha
Khudaa Ke Fazl Se Aamir Kahaa'n Buraa Hoga
