
Mere Amal Ka Na Maloom Kya Sila Hoga
Jahannami Bhi Koi Mujh Se Kya Bura Hoga

Amal Ye Kuch Nahi Daar-O-Madar Rehmat Ka
Tumhare Fazl-o-Karam Se Mera Bhala Hoga

Mila Jo Kuch Hai Woh Tere Karam Ka Sadqa
Hai Wagarna Hum Se Faqeeron Ka Haal Kya Hoga

Madar-e-Zindagi Zaahir Mein Bas Hawa Par Hai
Ye Ruk Gayi To Koi Lamha Bhar Jiya Hoga

Agar Na Shams-O-Qamar Hon Agar Na Hon Taare
Jale Ga Kya Vo Jo Makhlooq Ka Diya Hoga

Hazaar Baar Burai Ki Maine Koshish Ki
Tera Karam Jo Bachaye Toh Kab Bura Hoga

Jahaa'n Ne Iska Harr Ek Taur Se Buraa Chaha
Khudaa Ke Fazl Se Aamir Kahaan Buraa Hoga
