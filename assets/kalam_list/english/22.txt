
Mujhe Yaad Hai Tumhari 
Meri Tumse Lau Lagi Hai
Kabhi Mein Sunaa Raha Hun 
Kabhi Tumne Khud Suni Hai

Mai Ne Maasiva Ko Dekha 
Wahaa'n Sarr Basar Hai Dhoka
Tumhe Jab Bhi Hai Pukara 
Meri Har Duaa Suni Hai

Meri Zindagi Hai Tumse 
Meri Zindagi Mein Tum Hoo
Jo Tumhari Yaad Mein Hoo 
Yahi Zauqh-E-Zindagi Hai

Jo Hai Sab Ko Bhul Jataa 
Vo Khudaa Ko Yaad Karta
Jo Imam Bann Gaya Hai 
Usse Fikr-E-Mukhtadee Hai

Jo Tu Ilm Mein Phasaa Hai 
Na Samajh Tu Ba Khudaa Hai
Tu Jo Ilm Apna Samjha Toh 
Ye Ain-E-Mushrikee Hai

Tere Dil Mein Khalq Nafaay 
Kahi'n Zaar Bann Gayi Hai
Yeh Birhamee Nahi Hai 
Yeh Toh Teri Aazree Hai

Meri Shaan Aashiqana 
Meri Chaal Aajizana 
Mein Inhi Ka Hun Deewana 
Ye Adayae Aakhri Hai

Yeh Jahaa'n Ke Sab Manazir 
Hain Nazar Mein Aate Jaate
Mujhe Ab Toh Inn Mein  
Muthlaq Nazar Aati Khalqeee Hai

Mai Kareem Unko Bolun 
Ke Raheem Unko Bolun
Ussi Aan Unki Mujh Ko 
Nazar Aati Raahimi Hai

Mai Jahaa'n Ke Maalikon Ko 
Kahin Kab Nazar Mein Lataa
Innhe Aainoon Mein Uski 
Nazar Aati Maaliki Hai

Na Mai Shayari Ke Layaqh 
Na Mai Shayaroon Mein Daakhil
Mai Likhaya Ja Raha Hun 
Yahi Meri Shayari Hai

Yeh Taayunaata-E-A'alam 
Koi Shakl Hoo Ya Aadam
Wahi Ek Haqeeqat Inn 
Mein Ba Libaas-E-Zaahiri Hai

Kahin Saaz Mein Hai Tu 
He Kahi'n Soz Mein Hai Tu He
Mein Jahaa'n Jahaa'n Bhi 
Dekhun Teri Shaan Ek Nayi Hai

Mujhe Jo Mila Mila Hai 
Mujhe Tu Ne Sab Diya Hai
Mujhe Iss Se Badh Ke Kya 
Hai Teri Dosti Mili Hai

Mujhe Ab Jahaa'n Se Kuch 
Bhi Nahi Vastaa Raha Hai
Mai Tujhi Ko Dekhta Hun 
Meri Chashma-E-Dil Khuli Hai

Mera Raasta Yaqeenann 
Mere Nooria Hai Tujh Se
Issi Raah Mein Ali Hai 
Issi Raah Mein Nabi Hai

Tujhe Darr He Kya Hai Aamir 
Tujhe Raah Voh Mili Hai
Jahaa'n Par Vali Ali Hai 
Jahaan Haqq Bhi Hai Nabi Hai
