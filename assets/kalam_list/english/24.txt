Jo Rahaa Hoo Tere Khayal Mein
Usse Chaein Hai Na Qarar Hai
Na Kisi Ki Baat Mein Zauqh Hai
Na Kisi Pe Iska Madaar Hai

Mere Dil Ki Baat Mai Kya Kahun
Tu Aleem-E-Zaat-E-Sadvarr Hai
Mere Paas Tere Siva Hai Kya
Meri Jaa'n Bhi Tujh Pe Nisaar Hai

Kise Kya Khabar Ke Mai Kya Hun Ab
Mera Haal-E-Dil Huwa Kya Hai Ab
Tu Aleem Hai Tu Khabeer Hai
Tu He Saath Lail Vo Nihaar Hai

Tere Ishq Ka Mai Mareez Hun
Ussi Aag Ka Mai Hareeq Hun
Koi Kya Illaj Mera Kare
Tere Ishq Ka Ye Buqhaar Hai

Mai Pukara Unko Toh Mil Gaya
Mai Raha Nahi'n Wahi Reh Gaya
Meri Fikr Mujhse Judaa Huwi
Mujhe Yaar He Ka Khumaar Hai

Inhe Dekhte He Guzar Huwi
Inhe Sunte Sunte Basar Huwi
Rahi Jo Idhar Vo Udhar Huwi
Jo Udhar Huwi Vo Baekaar Hai

Mujhe Khalq Jaanti Kya Hai Ab
Mujhe Isse Vaasta Kya Hai Ab
Mujhe Kaam Hai Toh Tujhe Se Hai
Tujhe Chordh Dun Toh Tabaar Hai

Hoo Majaaz Tum Hoo Libaaz Tum
Banne Jism Tum Mera Dil Hoo Tum
Meri Jaan Tum Meri Shaan Tum
Ye Wazifa Lail-O-Nihaar Hai

Yahaa'n Farsh Par Bhi Tumhi Rahe
Wahaa'n Bhi Ba Arsh-E-Barri Rahe
Jahaa'n Bhi Rahe Tumhi Rahe
Ye Tumhe Se Garm Bazar Hai

Mujhe Dekh Ne Ko Toh Aamira
Tujhe Aaine Mile Ja Bajaa
Mera Rukh Numa Wahi Aaina
Hai Jo Noori Lahaa Azaar Hai
