
Ghaflat Se Nikal Kuch Kaam Bhi Kar
Hona Jo Na Tha Voh Hoo Bhi Gaya
Iss Zindagi Ka Sab Sarmaya Khona 
Jo Na Tha Voh Kho Bhi Gaya


Iss A'alam-E-Faani Mein Baba 
Rehne Ki Nahin Hai Koi Jagah
Bae Ghar Bhi Gaya Bae Zarr Bhi Gaya 
Jo Sahib-E-Zarr Tha Voh Bhi Gaya

Iss Zindagi Ke Auraaq Ulat Aur
Dekh Kya Hai Kya Abtak
Ghaflat Ke Tou Din Ghaflat Mein 
Kate Hoshiyar Hua Tou So Bhi Gaya

Badkaar He Kya Dukh Paate Hain 
Dukh Dard Mein Nek-O-Kaar Bhi Hain
Taqdeer Mein Jo Kuch Likha Hai 
Hoga Bhi Wahi Aur Hoo Bhi Gaya

Harr Maal Yahan Ka Faani Hai 
Zumaal Talak Bhi Faani Hai
Hatim He Se Kya Jaata Hai Miyaa'n 
Khaaroon Banna Tha Voh Bhi Gaya

Naam Uska Bhi Hai Aur Uska Bhi 
Dono Bhi Gaye Aur Daulat Bhi
Ek Naam Par Rehmat Hoti Hai 
Ek Maujib-E-Laanat Hoo Bhi Gaya

Karta Hai Ibadat Toh Apne 
Zimme Se Tu Farigh Hota Hai
Khairaat Mein Teri Nisbat Se 
Rabb Zimme Se Farigh Hoo Bhi Gaya

Tujh Se Ba-Tufail-E-Noori Ab Kya
Hoo Nahi Sakta Kya Na Hua
Kya Shaan Hai Teri Aye Aamir 
Karta Bhi Nahi Aur Hoo Bhi Gaya
