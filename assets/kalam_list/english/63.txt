
Hum Ko Baatein Banani To Aati Nahi 
Baat Ab Khalq Ki Dil Ko Bhaati Nahi
Shakl-o-Soorat Mein Jab Yaar He Yaar Hai 
Uss Ki Surat Nigahoo'n Se Jaati Nahi

Lakh Makhlooq Khenche Khinchaye Toh 
Kya Ab Ada Khalq Ki Dil Lubhati Nahi
Saath Ho Kar Hazaron Ke Phir Ek Hai 
Sab Adam Hai Wajood Unka Zaati Nahi

Dil To Harr Dam Tumhara Fidaai Hua
Vo Mohabbat Hai Dil Mein Jo Jaati Nahi
Ek Lamha Bhi Hum Tum Se Ghafil Nahi 
Duniya Aati Hai Tum Ko Bhulati Nahi

Lakh Aajaye Dunya-O-Maafiha Sab Dil 
Mein Mutlaq Jagah Kuch Bhi Paati Nahi
Hoon Sarasar Khata Kaar-o-Badkaar Mai 
Phir Bhi Umeed-E-Rehmat Toh Jaati Nahi

Chaien Dil Ka Gaya Aur Sukoon Bhi Gaya 
Raat Aati Hai Par Neend Aati Nahi
Rote Rote Guzarti Hai Ab Zindagi 
Ab Adaaye Dua Bhi Toh Aati Nahi

Sarr Uthaya Toh Noori Piya Aagaya 
Aur Kaha Aayi Rehmat Kya Aati Nahi
Aamir Aisa Pareshaa Kyun Hogaya 
Mujh Se Karwa Dua Tujh Ko Aati Nahi
