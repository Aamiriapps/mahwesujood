
Bazaahir Jee Raha Hun 
Darr Haqeeqat Marr Raha Hun Mai
Maraa Khud Se Magar Unse 
Yaqenann Je Raha Hun Mai

Mujhe Marna He Zaiba Hai
Inhe Jeena He Zaiba Hai
Issi Baai's Inhi Par Raat 
Din Ab Marr Raha Hun Mai

Mai Aaina Hu Unka
Dekhne Waalo'n Mujhe Dekho
Yaqenann Ainama Ka Aaina 
Hun Haqq Numa Hun Mai

Jo Raah-E-Haqq Mai Maraa 
Jaye Voh Marr Nahi Sakta
Ye Bal Ahya Ki Manzil Hai
Ke Jis Mein Jee Raha Hun Mai

Na Banda Hun Ke Jaise
Tum Samajte Hoo Mujhe Banda
Issi Se Hu Issi Ka Hun
Issi Par Ab Fidaa Hun Mai

Fanna Meri Baqaa Uski
Mili Mujh Ko Baqaa Uski
Fanaa Hote Huwe Bhi 
Aaj Tak Ain-E-Baqaa Hun Mai

Na Apno Ne Mujhe Samjha
Na Ghairoo'n Ne Mujhe Dekha
Khudaa He Jaanta Hai Reh
Gaya Hun Ya Gaya Hun Mai

Mera Maslak Nirala Hai
Meri Hasrat Nirali Hai
Khudaa He Par Khudaa He Par
Khudaa He Par Fidaa Hun Mai

Khudaa Se Maang Ne Waale
Khudaa Se Maangta Kya Hai
Khudaa He Ko Khudaa He Ko
Khudaa Se Maangta Hun Mai

Bila Chaabi Ke Aana Mutlaqann
Jayaz Nahi Rakha
Hai Chaabi Nisbat-E-Shaikhi
Issi Se Khul Raha Hun Mai

Samaj Chaabi Hai Noori Aur
Quraa'n Qufull Hai Uska
Toh Aamir Isse Khule Ja
Khuloo'n Ga Aur Khula Hun Mai
