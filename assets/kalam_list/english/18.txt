
Pardaa Karte Bhi Hoo 
Aur Parde Ke Bahar Bhi Hoo
Kya Ajab Hai Ke Tumhien 
Baatin-O-Zaahir Bhi Hoo

Tumko Ab Kaun Se Aazmaa’n 
Mein Dhoondo'n Maula
Tum Toh Harr Waqt Se 
Awwal Bhi Hoo Aakhir Bhi Hoo

Tum Jo Dene He Pe 
Aajaao Koi Kya Roke
Lene Walaa Koi Momin 
Hoo Ya Kaafir Bhi Hoo

Na Rahaa Khauf-O-Khatar 
Dono Jahaa'n Ka Mutlaqh
Kyun Ki Tum Ghaafir-O
-Raahim Bhi Hoo Naasir Bhi Hoo

Mai Toh Aalim Bhi Nahi 
Aamil-E-Saleh Bhi Nahi
Itna Bhi Bas Hai Mera 
Sarr Tere Darr Par Bhi Hoo

Meri Batoon Ki Haqeeqat
Koi Mujh Se Puche
Kyun Na Harr Baat Ajab 
Hoo Meri Nadir Bhi Hoo

Jab Haqeeqat Meri Mujh 
Ko Nazar Aajati Hai
Iss Se Bhi Baadke Mai'n 
Batarr Hoon Jo Batarr Bhi Hai

Mujh Se Afzal He Nazar 
Aatha Hai Zarra Zarra
Kaam Ki Cheez Samajhta 
Hoon Jo Patharr Bhi Hoo

Un Ki Jab Zaath Pe Jaati 
Hain Nigahien Meri
Tab Muqhabil Mein Bataaon 
Koi Behtar Bhi Hoo?

Bas Tumhare He Karam 
Ka Hai Bhikaari Aamir
Koi Hoo Kuch Bhi Hoo Faasiq 
Bhi Hoo Faajir Bhi Hoo
