
Jidhar Dekho Udhar Jalwa 
Numa Allah He Allah Hai
Zamee'n-O-Asamaa'n Mein 
Barrmala Allah He Allah Hai

Na Saaqi Hai Na Sagar Hai
Na Mai'n Hai Aur Na Mai Khana
Ye Aouhaamei'n Jahaa'n Faani 
Baqaa Allah He Allah Hai

Ye Ajsaam-E-Zamee'n Saare
Voh Ajraam-E-Falak Saare
Ye Sab Hain Naam Ke Dhoke
Raha Allah He Allah Hai

Zameeni Hoo Samaai Hoo 
Ya Ma Fil Beher Maahi Hoo
Har Ek Mein Har Tarha Se
Barmala Allah He Allah Hai

Marizoo'n Dhoonte Kya Hoo
Tabbiboo Sochte Kya Hoo
Dawa Allah He Allah Hai
Shafaa Allah He Allah Hai

Faza Ke Kaifiyat Kya Hai
Hawa Ke Maahiyat Kya Hai
Faza Allah He Allah Hai
Hawa Allah He Allah Hai

Jahaan Waloo'n Tumhe Jo Kuch
Mila Tumko Mubarak Hoo
Mujhe Ab Tak Mila Hai Toh
Mila Allah He Allah Hai

Nahi Hokar Bhi Hona Kya Hai
Bas Iss Ko Samajh Lena
Bas Ek Adam-E-Izaafi Ke 
Siva Allah He Allah Hai

Har Ek Shaey Vaaste Tere
Magar Tu Vaaste Kiske
Teri Iss Zindagi Ka Muduaa
Allah He Allah Hai

Huwa Aafaq Mein Anta 
Huzoori Mein Ana Khud Mein
Bas Aafaq Aur Anfus Mein
Raha Allah He Allah Hai

Zuhoor Ghaib Mein Uske
Siva Koi Nahi Milta
Khula Allah He Allah Hai
Chupa Allah He Allah Hai

Ye Manzil Kya Ye Maqsad Kya 
Safar Kya Aur Iqamath Kya
Jo Maqfi Tha Wahi Zaahir Huwa
Allah He Allah Hai

Kahin Bill Faiel Zaahir Hai
Kahin Bill Quwa Qaayim Hai
Kahin Raazi Kahin Bae Irtizaa
Allah He Allah Hai
