
Mere Haal-E-Zaar Pe Aye Khudaa
Tera Fazl Hota He Jayega
Koi Saath Hoo Ke Na Hoo Mere
Mera Muduaa Toh Baraye Ga

Mujhe Zaat-E-Khalq Ka Hai Pataa
Ke Hamesha Gham Ki Hai Ye Ghataa
Jo Khudaa Ki Yaad Mai Hai Sadaa
Usse Kaise Chaein Na Aayega

Mujhe Azm Hai Na He Jasm Hai
Na He Zabd Hai Na Qarar Hai
Tune Harr Tarah Se Bachaa Liya 
Tu He Harr Tarah Se Bachaye Ga

Mere Khaaliqa Mere Maalika
Mere Raaziqa Mere Qaadira
Tu Na De Toh Phir Zaraa Ye
Bataa Koi Hai Jo Deta Bhi Jayega

Tu Kareem Hai Tu Raheem Hai
Tu Ghani Hai Aur Hameed Hai
Mujhe Hai Yaqeen Mere Maalika
Tu He Hai Dega Aur Dilaye Ga

Teri Izzatoo'n Ki Khasam Mujhe
Teri Azmatoo'n Ki Khasam Mujhe
Tere Darr Se Uth Ke Tera Gadaa
Kisi Aur Darr Pe Na Jayega

Tune Aaj Tak Bhi Diya Mujhe
Kisi Kaam Se Kisi Naam Se
Inn He Parda-E-Majaaz Ko
Jo Uthaye Ga Tujhe Paayega

Mujhe Tang Kar Diya Ishq Ke
Kahi'n Jazb Ne Kahi'n Kaif Ne
Ke Mai Phaardunga Hijaab Toh
Tu Kahaan Chupega Chupayega

Mai Rahun Lutun Ke Jiyun Marun
Mujhe Iss Se Vaaste Kuch Nahi
Miyaa'n Tum Mile Toh Hai Sab Mila
Koi Kya Milega Milayegaa

Mai Haqeer Masht-E-Turaab Tha
Yun Kahun Ke Khanaa Kharab Tha
Mujhe Jo Bhi Tune Banna Diya
Koi Hai Jo Aisa Bannaye Ga

Mujhe Paal Na Tera Kaam Hai
Mai Jiyun Toh Tera He Naam Hai
Ke Jo Kha Raha Hai Vo Abd Hai
Toh Kahan Kisi Ko Khilaye Ga

Maine Ek Rukh Toh Bataa Diya
Mujhe Issmein Paayega Lapata
Mera Doosra Hai Pataa Mujhe
Sagye Darr Tu Noori Ka Payega

Darr-E-Khanqah Pe Tu Aamira
Baardi Mudatoo'n Se Hai Kyun Khada
Mai Ye Chahatahoo'n Mere Piya
Tere Dil Mein Ghar Banna Jayega
