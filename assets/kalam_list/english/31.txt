
Tum Agar Mere Raho Bas 
Mujhe Hajat Kya Hai
Iss Se Baar'dh Kar Meri Jaa'n 
Phir Mujhe Rahat Kya Hai

Tum Agar Saath Raho
Toh Mujhe Kis Shaey Ki Kami
Phir Safar Kya Hai Hazar
Kya Hai Iqamath Kya Hai

Tum Ko Rakte Huwe Ghairoo'n 
Ke Taraf Rukh Karna
Iss Se Baar'dh Kar Ba Khudaa 
Koi Himakhat Kya Hai

Jo Diya Tumne Diya Aaj 
Talak Kam Na Kiya
Iss Se Baar'dh Kar Meri 
Jaa'n Aur Saqawath Kya Hai

Nehnu Aqrabuu Ke Sadaa 
Jab Se Suni Hai Maine
Tum Pe Qurbaa'n Hai Meri
Jaan Ye Qurbath Kya Hai

Kaun Jaane Mujhe Jaano 
Toh Tumhi Ek Jaano
Zindagi Mein Meri Ab 
Khalwath-O-Jalwath Kya Hai

Sair-E-Gulshan Mein Bhi Hoo'n 
Khaid-E-Nashemann Bhi Hoo'n 
Rahat-E-Sair Hai Kya
Khaid Ki Halat Kya Hai

Ab Tere Waqt Pe Bhi
Khaid Lagi Hai Aamir
Jaane De Chor'dh Bhi De
Toul Ki Aadat Kya Hai
