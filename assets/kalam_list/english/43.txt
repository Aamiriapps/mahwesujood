
Apni Nisbath Aur Hai
Fazl-E-Khudaa Kuch Aur Hai
Aaj Tak Kuch Aur Thi 
Ab Toh Khizaa Kuch Aur Hai

Aaj Tak Bhi Iss Jahaa'n Mein 
Naam Ke Charche Rahe
Darr Haqeeqat Karne Waale
Ka Pataa Kuch Aur Hai

Ghaflatoo'n Mein Zindagi 
Maine Guzaari Aaj Tak
Jab Khuli Aankhein Toh Dekha
Maajra Kuch Aur Hai

Mera Tera Mein Koi Hai
Aur Mai Tu Mein Koi
Ibdiyaa Kuch Aur Hai
Aur Muduaa Kuch Aur Hai

Zaahirann Kuch Aur Hai
Aur Baatinann Kuch Aur Hai
Deedae Haqq Baien Mai
Sultan-O-Gadaa Kuch Aur Hai

A'alam-E-Duniya Hai Saara
Ranj-O-Gham Mein Mubtala
Kar Rahe Hai Kuch Dawa
Lekin Dawa Kuch Aur Hai

Tu Usse Bhula Magar Uss Ne
Bulaya Kab Tujhe 
Zaat Teri Aur Hai Tera
Khudaa Kuch Aur Hai

Zikr Bhi Hoo Istebaaq-E
-Khair Bhi Hota Rahe
Aamir Vo Noori Ki Ye Tarz-E
-Dua Kuch Aur Hai
