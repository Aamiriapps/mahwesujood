
Dil Mein Tumhari Yaad Hoo
Lab Pe Tumhara Naam Hoo
Dhun Yahi Harr Ghadi Rahe
Subha Hoo Yaake Sham Hoo

Chaein Hai Aur Sukoo'n Mera
Jis Mein Hoo Tera Tazkira
Taleem-E-Ilm-E-Khaas Hoo 
Ya Vo Bayaan-E-Aam Hoo

Meri Namaz Ki Khabar Kya 
Jaane Koi Bae Khabar
Mai'n Hatt Gaya Vo Reh Gaye
Sajda Hoo Ya Qiyaam Hoo

Ek Lamha Bhi Toh Yaad Mein
Ya Dil Laga De Zaat Mein
Bas Itna Bhi Jo Kar Liya
Tu Faaiez Al Maraam Hoo

Mai Hun Asaalatann Turab
Garr Che Hun Khasta-O-Kharab
Par Mere Dam Se Maalikaa
Tera He Eheteraam Hoo

Mujhsa Bura Koi Nahi'n
Tujhsa Bhala Koi Nahi'n
Ek Saans Bhi Na Le Sakun
Garr Tera Inteqaam Hoo

Dil Chahta Hai Ab Mera
Shaha Tujhi Par Hoo Fidaa
Har Lamhe Mein Hazar Baar
Tujhpe He Marna Kaam Hoo

Aankhon Ka Noor Tu He Hai
Saara Zuhoor Tu He Hai
Zaahir Mein Tera Naam Hoo
Baatin Mein Tera Kaam Hoo

Jab Tune Sarr Jhuka Diya
Uthne Ka Izteraab Kya
Jab Vo Uthayenge Tujhe
Toh Sajda Tamam Hoo

Aakhir-E-Kaar De Ke Jab
Noori Piya Ka Vaasta
Roote Huwe Kaha Shaha
Mera Koi Maqam Hoo

Iss Ne Kaha Ke Maalika 
Mere Liye Tu Sunn Dua
Tere Karam Mein Shah-E-Mann
Mera Bhi Ek Ghulam Hoo

Tu Kehdiya Tu Hai Mera
Tere Liye Suni Dua
Aamir Ke Vaaste Tera 
Daman He Ek Maqam Hai
