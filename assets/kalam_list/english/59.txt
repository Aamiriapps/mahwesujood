
Fazl He Ka Natija Hai Jo Bhi Huwa
Warna Hum Se Bhala Haye Kya Hoo Sake
Jo Huwa Voh Tumhare Karam Se Huwa
Bas Sitam Ke Siwa Humse Kya Hoo Sake

Dostoo Kan Kholo Zarra Sun Bhi Lo
Dosti Bhi Yaqenann Tumhari Nahi'n
Unki Chahat Se Tum Chahte Hoo Mujhe
Warna Malum Hai Tumse Kya Hoo Sake

Mai Agar Sochta Hu'n Toh Hai'n Aaftaei'n 
Khud Ko Jab Dekhta Hu'n Toh Hai Zehmatei'n
Tum Mere Saath Hoo Toh Mujhe Kya Kami
Harr Musibat Yaqenann Hawa Hoo Sake

Tu Mere Saath Hai Aur Mere Paas Hai
Tujhse Qaayim Yaqenann Meri Zaat Hai
Aise Surat Mai'n Mujhse Bhala Kya Na Hoo
Kyun Na Shahoo'n Se Uncha Gadaa Hoo Sake

Aamira Fazl-E-Haqq Se Tujhe Mil Gaya
Ba Tufail-E-Rasool-E-Khudaa Muda'aa
Hai Waseela Tera Jab Ke Noori Piya
Kyun Na Phir Teri Hajat Rawaa Hoo Sake
