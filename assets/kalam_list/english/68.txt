
Bahut Kuch Kho Diya Jo Hai
Ghaneemat Jaan Aye Baba 
Kiya Hai Kya Tha Karna Kya
Yeh Ab Pehchaan Aye Baba

Gaye Aage Bahut Kuch Aur 
Bahut Jaate Nazar Aaye 
Musafir Hoo Ke Manzil Se 
Hai Kyun Anjaan Aye Baba

Kahin Shaadi Kisi Ki Hai 
Kahin Pehlu Mein Barbaadi 
Yeh Duniya Hai Badalti 
Har Dam-O-Harr Aan Aye Baba

Kahin Hasna Kahin Rona 
Kahin Ro Ro Ke Chup Rehna 
Yeh Hai Ahwal-E-Duniya Harr 
Ghadi Harr Aan Aye Baba

Kisi Ne Ibtida Se Intiha 
Tak Gham Nahin Jaana 
Kisi Ke Dil Mein Saare Reh
Gaye Armaan Aye Baba

Gharaz Duniya Se Dil Kya 
Hai Lagaya Pher Le Isko
Yahi Hai Raaz-E-Panah 
Aur Yahi Irfan Aye Baba

Ye Duniya Cheez Hai Kya 
Is Jahaan Se Le Ke Jana Kya
Ye Tan Tak Choot Jayega 
Yaqeenann Jaan Aye Baba

Bhula De Za'am Ko Apne 
Mita De Weham Ko Apne
Bana La Na'am Ko Apne 
Ana Ko Jaan Aye Baba

Tu Kya Jaane Ga Aamir 
Tujh Ko Kya Tha Janna Aata
Ye Sab Noori Piya Ka Tujh 
Mein Hai Faizan Aye Baba
