Ye Zindagi Tumhari Hai
Tum Parr Fidaa Rahe
Harr Dum Tumhari Yaad
Rahe Tazkira Rahe

Tum Dil Mein Hoo Makee'n
Toh Kahun Laa Makaa'n Kyun
Itna Paate Jisse Bhi Rahe
Baa Khudaa Rahe

Ab Tu Zameen Hai Na
Kahin Aasmaa'n Hai
Ghair-E-Khudaa Nazar Se
 Haatee Bas Khudaa Rahe

Surat Badal Badal Ke 
Bhi Aao To Kya Huwa
Harr Martabae Fanna Mein
Tumhari Baqaa Rahe

Bas Jism Mein Tumhi Ho Meri 
Jaan-O-Dill Mein Tum
Dil Mein Tumhari Yaad
Zabaa'n Par Nidaa Rahe

Aise Shafiq Tum Ho 
Ke Tumsa Koi Nahi'n
Bhule Huwoo'n Ke Yaad
Mein Bhi Tum Sadaa Rahe

Dushmann Tumhare Hain Bhi 
Toh Tumse He Palte Hain
Tum Jitna Inke Saath Rahe
Vo Judaa Rahe

Tum Ho Kareem Aur
Ghafoor-O-Raheem Ho
Iss Badaa Gareeb Ko
Kya Aasraa Rahe

Apne Karam Se Tumne
Hume Shaad Kar Diya
Mehrum Kab Karam Se
Tumhara Gadaa Rahe

Imaan Aur Amal Pe Rakha 
Hai Madare Dee'n 
Ye Hai Kamale Deen
Ke Fazl-E-Khudaa Rahe

Iss Se Ziyada Aur Kahun 
Kya Bataoon Mai'n 
Iss Tarah Hoo Fanah 
Ke Na Ma-O-Shumaa Rahe

Asrar Hai'n Kahin Toh
Yu'n Ehkaam Hai Kahin
Inn Mein Khudaa Rahe 
Toh Wahaan Mustafa Rahe

Aamir Ki Kya Bisaath
Hai Asrar Jo Kahe
Noori Piya Ke Samne
Vo Aainaa Rahe
