Khud Se Hum Khud Kho Gaye 
Kya Kar Diya Ek Baat Mein
Saraa A'alam Hatt Ke Ya’n 
Se Reh Gayaa Uss Zaath Mein

Jo Rahaa Bas Woh Rahaa 
Ab Mai Rahaa Na Tu Rahaa
Bach Gaye Rehne Se Hum 
Harr Baat Ki Aafaath Mein

Wehm Ki Jab Thi Khudi 
Harr Aan Hoti Thi Badi'i
Ab Toh Uss Jamaay Mein 
Hain Kyun Bheegtae Barsaat Mein

Laa Se Sab Kuch Cheen Kar 
Illa Se Sab Kuch De Diya
Humko Kya Se Kya Kiya 
Bas Ek Nafi'i Iss Baat Mein

Wehm Ka Pardaa Utha Toh 
Khud Numayaa Hogaye
Ab Vo Humko Harr Jagah 
Aaye Nazar Din Raath Mein

Apni Hasthi Ka Gumaa’n Tha 
Hum Kahan Maujood Thae
Ilm Ke Naqshae Thae Uss Ke 
Aur Basea Thae Zaath Mein

Dour Karde Wehm Apna 
Toh Khudaa Tujhko Mile
Phir Teri Manzil Pe Manzil 
Hai Sifaath-O-Zaath Mein

Haqq Yeh Bole Mangta 
Kya Hai Bataa Tu Aamira
Vaasta Deta Hai Jo Noori 
Ka Tu Harr Baat Mein
