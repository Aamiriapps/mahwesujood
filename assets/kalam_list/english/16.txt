
Kya Ajab Hai Sab Mein 
Reh Kar Sab Se Phir Tanha Bhi Hai
Aankh Se Ghayeb Bhi Hai 
Aur Phir Nazar Aata Bhi Hai

Jo Hai Mangta Wo Danee Hai 
Jo Hai Dataa Voh Ghani
Harr Taraf Jalwanumaa Mangta 
Bhi Hai Dataa Bhi Hai

Hai Munazza Wasf-E-Bashari Se 
Yaqenann Zaat-E-Haqq
Sarhad-E- Tashbiya Mein 
Banda Bhi Hai Maula Bhi Hai

Zaat Laasaani Hai Teri 
Shaan Subhani Teri
Ba Wajood Iske Hamare 
Saath Tu Rehta Bhi Hai

Seed-E-Maahi Ke Liye Jataa 
Hai Koi Beher Tak
Fazl Hoo Toh Le Ke Maahi 
Aagayaa Dariya Bhi Hai

Ghafiloo Karte Hoo Kya 
Kehte Hoo Kis Se Hajatie'n
Khalqh Mein Itna Toh Bolo 
Koi Ek Shunwa Bhi Hai

Ek Taraf Ghaalib Hun Sab 
Par Ek Taraf Aajiz Hun Mein
Ek Taraf Mujh Mein Hai Dariya 
Ek Taraf Khatra Bhi Hai

Ek Taraf Kuch Bhi Nahi Hun 
Neasth Hun Naabood Hun
Ek Taraf Rukh Sab Mein A'alaa 
Hoo Ke Phir Adnaa Bhi Hai

Ek Taraf Aamir Hai Khaak-E
-Paaye Noori Bill Yaqeen
Ek Taraf Adnaa Hai Aisa 
Har Taraf A'alaa Bhi Hai
