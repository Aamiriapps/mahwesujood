
Na Dekhe Huwe Jo Hai Deewane Unke
Agar Dekhlenge Toh Kya Kya Karenge
Khudaa Aur Muhammed Ke Jalwe Hai Saare
Yahi Dekhte Hai Dikhaya Karenge

Hoo Noor-E-Nazar Tum Hoo Ain-E-Nazar Tum
Hoo Naazir Mein Tum Aur Manzarr Mein Bhi Tum
Hoo Manzoor Ab Tum Na Kuch Dour Ab Tum
Tumhara Harr Ek Suu Nazara Karenge

Jo Zakhm-E-Jigar Humne Khaye Hai Ab Tak
Kahan Vo Kisi Ko Dikhaye Hai'n  Ab Tak
Tumhare He Rehm-O-Karam Ka Hoo Marham
Tumhi Ko Jigar Hum Dikhaya Karenge

Ye A'alam Agar Mil Gaya Bhi Toh Kya Hai
Mai Samjhu'n Ke Andhe Ko Manzarr Mila Hai
Charaagh-E-Hidayat Jinhe Mil Gaya Hai
Jidhar Rukh Karenge Ujala Karenge

Tujhe Ainama Ki Sunayi Hai Aayat 
Fasamaa Mein Tujh Ko Dikhayi Hai Surat
Tu Paar'dh Ta Toh Hai Dekhta Kyun Nahi'n Hai
Idhar Aa Tujhe Hum Dikahaya Karenge

Tawakul Ki Manzil Mein Maine Hai Dekha 
Tamasha Ajab Inki Razzaqiyath Ka
Vo Haatoo'n Se Apne Khilate Hai Mujhko 
Agar Tum Bhi Aao Khilaya Karenge

Nahi'n Hai'n Amal Kuch Dikhane Ke Khaabil
Agar Hai'n Amal Toh Chupane Ke Khaabil
Magar Ek Tu Hai Dikhane Ke Khaabil
Tujhe Nooria Hum Dikhaya Karenge

Issi Parr Khudaa Bhi Kahe Aamira Ab
Tera Paar Baeda Kya Hum Ne Jaa Bas
Ali Honge Uss Dam Muhammed Bhi Honge 
Khudaa Se Mujhe Voh Milaya Karenge
